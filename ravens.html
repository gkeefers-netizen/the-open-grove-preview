New from the grove — Odin's Ravens

Three new designs, drawn from the old poems and the old Icelandic staves. The ravens are Grímnismál's; the Vegvísir is a 19th-century Icelandic wayfinding stave. In Grímnismál, Huginn and Muninn fly over the world each day; Snorri adds that they come home to speak all the tidings into Odin's ear. These are the ravens, as we imagined them.

New design

Ravens around the Vegvísir

Two ravens circling the wayfinding stave, ringed with knotwork. For finding your way home — and remembering who flew with you.
