The Wheel of the Year

Eight festivals mark the turning seasons — solstices, equinoxes, and the cross-quarter days between them. Not every pagan keeps all eight, and Heathens, Druids, and others keep their own calendars besides. But this wheel is the rhythm many of us share. Tap a festival to walk through its season. Dates below are for the Northern Hemisphere; south of the equator, the wheel turns six months opposite — use the hemisphere switch below to see your own dates.
